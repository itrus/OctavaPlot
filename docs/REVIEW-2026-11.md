# Source review of 14 November 2026: the findings and what became of them

Six readers each read one part of the source and wrote a report (the model core, the first and second halves of `model/graph`, the state core, the statistics, and the utilities and data code). Each finding was proved by a throwaway test or by tracing the code. The reports (170 kB) and the throwaway tests were kept in a scratch folder of the session and are not part of the repository; this file keeps what is worth keeping: every finding, its severity, and where it was dealt with. The code is the reference now; the BACKLOG change log ("14 Nov 2026 (audit)", the rows of lists B to E and I) says what each change was.

A reader did not repeat what BACKLOG already listed. The single-reader limit stands: nothing here was read twice, and the parts of the source that no one has read are in the tidy-up list (G), not here.

Status: **fixed (audit)** = named in the BACKLOG row "14 Nov 2026 (audit)", matched by its wording and not looked at again in the code; **done** = done by the item of the tidy-up list named; **checked** = looked at again in the code on 14 November 2026 and found fixed. No finding of the six reports was found open. Two things that follow from findings are open and are in BACKLOG: the two degenerate five-parameter fits that are called converged (list I) and the type-only import cycles of the chart families (list D).
